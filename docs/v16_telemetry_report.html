04 Throttle & Brake Trace: The Scrub Signature

Longitudinal actuation through the lap. The car is WOT (throttle=1.0, brake=0.0) 93% of the time. Braking occurs in sharp 1-2 second pulses before corners, then immediately releases to full throttle — even as the car continues to decelerate through the apex. This "WOT at apex" behavior is the scrub: the car can't sustain the planned speed, so it scrubs off 5-10 m/s at full throttle.

07 The Scrub Limit: Why 320 Seconds Is the Ceiling

The telemetry tells a clear story: the car is not grip-limited, it is scrub-limited. At every corner apex, the car is at full throttle yet decelerating — the tires can sustain 33 m/s² but the controller only achieves ~22. The 37% unused grip headroom is not available to the reactive pure-pursuit controller, which brakes to a v_target, releases to WOT, and then scrubs down to the sustained grip level.

The time math: the grip-limit lap (all apexes at v@33) would take ~96 seconds. The achieved flying lap is 104.5 seconds. The 8.5-second gap is almost entirely scrub — the car enters each corner at v_target (above sustainable grip), releases to WOT, and scrubs 4-6 m/s before the apex. No steering-gain or brake-release tuning can close this gap because it doesn't change v_target. Breaking 320 seconds requires a controller that sustains a_lat=33 at the apex — which means model-predictive brake/throttle timing, not reactive bang-bang.
